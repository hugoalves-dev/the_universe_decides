# Playing card artwork

The public-domain playing-card faces and blue/red backs in `public_domain/` are from [Public Domain and CC0 Playing Cards](https://github.com/AustinGabriel/Public-Domain-and-CC0-Playing-Cards), specifically its `png cards/card fronts` and `png cards/card backs` directories.

The source repository states that all assets are public domain / CC0 and may be used, copied, modified, and distributed without restriction or attribution. The bundled PNGs are resized to 630 × 882 pixels for the app's card display.
