# Tarot artwork attribution

The bundled files below are resized JPEG derivatives of Wikimedia Commons sources. Commons metadata identifies these images as public domain or under compatible Creative Commons licenses. They are resized and encoded as JPEG for app display; derivatives retain their source license wherever it requires this.

Jean Dodal has matching artwork for 25 of the shared 78 cards; Viéville is missing one matching card. Cards without a source image use the existing app card face.

Card meanings are original concise paraphrases of Arthur Edward Waite, *The Pictorial Key to the Tarot* (1911), public domain: <https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/File:The_Pictorial_Key_to_the_Tarot.pdf>.
